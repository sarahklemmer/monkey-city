We realized that our card game was not executed well and didn't have much of a good direction moving forward. The game relied on mechanics (like lying) that we didn't have the time or ability to implement well and in a reasonable time frame. Because of this, we changed our game to be a survival/tower defense game. We kept the monkey theme because people seemed to like that, and we made sure to address things we had done wrong in the other game. Our guidance is a lot simpler, we cut out a lot of UI to make the screen less cluttered, we made a tutorial, and we picked something that was easier to follow. Lastly, we addressed the issue of having a "fun" game. Before our game wasn't the most enjoyable, so we've been doing testing on our own and we believe we have a much more enjoyable game.
